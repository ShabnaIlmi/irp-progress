3. Decisive turns

For each failed run, I went back one turn at a time, replaced the agent’s response with the correct one, and let it carry on. The earliest turn whose fix made the task succeed is the decisive turn.

Each square is one of the 708 failed runs. Purple squares got a decisive turn from replay. Grey squares had no single fixable turn, often because of two separate mistakes, so I left them unresolved instead of guessing.

decisive turn found, 300unresolved, 408

That is 42% coverage, and I report it next to every accuracy figure. Separately, five runs were excluded for context overflow, leaving 703 in the labelling pool. Six annotators now check sampled labels, three judgements per trace, without seeing the replay answer.

Only their results are used: the decisive-turn labels and the kept injections. With the 1,441 main runs (Qwen3-8B 800, Granite 160, Llama 160, Ministral 161, Qwen3.5-27B 160), this accounts for all 7,243 runs in the run index.
